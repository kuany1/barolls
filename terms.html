How ordering from BAROLLS works. Last updated 12 November 2026.

1. About these terms

These terms explain how ordering from BAROLLS works. By placing an order you agree to them. Nothing in these terms takes away any right you have under the laws of Uganda.

2. Seller information

Phone and WhatsApp: +256 765 068833.

3. How ordering works

  • Orders are placed through WhatsApp. You can pick items on the Order page or message us directly.
  • The Order page shows your items and total before you send anything. You can review your order, change it or leave without ordering at any point before you send it.
  • Your WhatsApp message is a request to buy. An order is only accepted when BAROLLS confirms it to you on WhatsApp. We may decline an order, for example if an item is unavailable or we are closed.
  • This website does not confirm orders automatically and does not take payments.

4. Availability

Menu items may sometimes be unavailable. If something you ordered is unavailable, we will tell you, and we will refund anything you have already paid for it.

5. Prices

Prices are in Ugandan shillings (UGX) and are shown on the menu. The total on the Order page is worked out from those prices. We confirm the final amount on WhatsApp. If a price on the website turns out to be wrong, we will tell you before we prepare your order. Any extra charge will be told to you before you confirm.

6. Payment

You do not pay on this website. We will tell you how to pay when we confirm your order.

7. Collection and delivery

Orders are for collection from our Arua location. We have not published a delivery service on this website. If we agree any other arrangement with you, we will confirm it on WhatsApp before you confirm your order. When we confirm your order we will tell you when to expect it to be ready. Times are estimates.

8. Changes and cancellations

If you need to change or cancel an order, message or call us as soon as you can. We will try to help if we have not started preparing your food. Once an item has been prepared we may not be able to cancel it, except where the law gives you that right.

9. Food, ingredients and allergies

Menu descriptions and photographs are summaries and are for illustration. If you have a food allergy or dietary requirement, tell us in your order and wait for our reply before you collect. Ask us about ingredients if you are unsure.

10. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of Uganda.

11. Contact

Questions about an order or these terms: +256 765 068833 (phone and WhatsApp).
