About BAROLLS

Chapati-wrapped Barolls, inspired by the locals.

Fast food in Arua, rolled to order

BAROLLS is a restaurant in Arua, Uganda. We specialise in Barolls — shawarma-style wraps rolled in chapati — and we also serve fries, snacks, teas and juices.

Every Baroll is rolled to order. You can browse the menu, order ahead on WhatsApp and collect from our location near Hill Main Roundabout on Pakwach Highway.
