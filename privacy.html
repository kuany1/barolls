Privacy Policy

How BAROLLS handles your information. Last updated 12 November 2026.

1. Who we are

This website belongs to BAROLLS, a restaurant at Plot No. 1 Staff Lane, Pakwach Highway, Hill Main Roundabout, next to Osgard Liquor Hub, Arua, Uganda. You can reach us on WhatsApp or by phone on +256 765 068833.

2. What this website does and does not collect

  • This website has no accounts, no online payments and no database.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and the website does not set cookies of its own.
  • On the Order page, the name, pickup time and instructions you type stay in your browser. They are only used to write your WhatsApp message. They are not sent to us until you tap Send inside WhatsApp.

3. Information we receive when you order

When you send an order or a message on WhatsApp, we receive what you send: your WhatsApp name and phone number, your name as you typed it, the items you ordered, any pickup time and special instructions, and the rest of the conversation.

We use this only to take and confirm your order, prepare it, answer your questions and keep ordinary business records. We do not sell your information, and we do not use it to send you marketing messages unless you ask us to.

4. WhatsApp and other services

  • WhatsApp. Messages are sent through WhatsApp, which is run by Meta. WhatsApp handles your information under its own terms and privacy policy, which we do not control.
  • Google Maps. The Contact page shows an embedded Google map. When that page loads, your browser connects to Google, which may collect information under Google's own policies. The “Get directions” link also opens Google Maps.
  • Website hosting. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and pages requested) to run and secure the service.

5. How long we keep information

We keep order conversations only for as long as we need them to handle your order, answer follow-up questions and keep ordinary business records. We have not yet set a fixed retention period. When we do, we will state it on this page.

6. Keeping information safe

We take reasonable care of the phone and WhatsApp account used to receive orders. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, so please do not put sensitive information (such as payment card details) in your order message.

7. Your rights

Uganda's Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 gives you rights over your personal data. In plain terms, you can:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is inaccurate, out of date or no longer needed;
  • ask us to stop using your information in a way that causes you harm or distress, or to stop using it for marketing; and
  • complain to the Personal Data Protection Office (PDPO) at NITA-U if you think your information has been mishandled.

To use any of these rights, message or call us using the details below. We may ask you to confirm who you are first.

8. Children

This website is not aimed at children. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to place orders for you.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
